# Elisabeth (Elle) Goldman, PhD

Computational biologist working on epigenetic aging and cancer genomics. I build models that turn DNA methylation and other multi-omic data into measures of biological age and cancer risk, and I bring an evolutionary lens to why those signals exist.

Most recently Computational Biologist III at OHSU's Cancer Early Detection Advanced Research Center (CEDAR). PhD in Biological Anthropology, University of Oregon.

**Current interests**

- Epigenetic clocks that generalize across tissues and species
- How single-cell chromatin changes with age, and how much of that signal survives real assays
- Leakage-aware validation for machine learning on omics data
- Evolutionary dynamics in tumors and clonal hematopoiesis
